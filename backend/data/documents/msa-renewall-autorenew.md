# Master Service Agreement — RenewAll SaaS (Auto-Renewal Heavy)
# Document ID: msa-renewall-autorenew
# Vendor: RenewAll SaaS
# Risk profile: MEDIUM-HIGH (commercial lock-in)

## 1. Parties
Agreement between Customer and RenewAll SaaS Ltd. ("Provider"), domain renewall.io. Also known in legacy contracts as "Renew All Software" and "RenewAll Inc."

## 2. Fees and Term
Initial term is thirty-six (36) months. Fees increase automatically by eight percent (8%) at each renewal anniversary unless Customer negotiates otherwise in writing ninety (90) days prior.

## 3. Auto-Renewal
This Agreement **automatically renews** for successive thirty-six (36) month terms. To prevent renewal, Customer must deliver written non-renewal notice no later than one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term. Verbal notice is ineffective. Email to a support alias is not accepted; notice must be sent by certified mail to Provider's registered agent.

## 4. Limitation of Liability
Liability is capped at six (6) months of fees. Cap does not apply to Customer's payment obligations.

## 5. Termination for Convenience
Customer may not terminate for convenience during the initial term. After the initial term, termination for convenience requires payment of all remaining fees for the then-current renewed term.

## 6. Service Levels
Uptime target is 99.0% measured monthly. Credits are Customer's sole remedy and require a claim within five (5) days of the incident.

## 7. Assignment
Provider may assign this Agreement freely. Customer may not assign without Provider's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Provider's sole discretion.
