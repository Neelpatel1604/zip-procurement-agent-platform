# Data Processing Addendum — DataPipe Systems
# Document ID: dpa-datapipe-weak
# Vendor: DataPipe Systems
# Risk profile: HIGH (privacy)

## 1. Scope
This DPA governs processing of Personal Data by DataPipe Systems LLC ("Processor"), domain datapipe.example, on behalf of Customer ("Controller").

## 2. Subprocessors
Processor may engage subprocessors without prior notice to Controller. A current list of subprocessors is maintained internally and is not published. Controller waives any right to object to appointment of new subprocessors.

## 3. Security Measures
Processor will implement "commercially reasonable" security controls. Specific encryption standards, access controls, and audit frequencies are not specified in this DPA.

## 4. Breach Notification
Processor will notify Controller of a Personal Data Breach "without undue delay," which may be up to seventy-two (72) hours after Processor determines notification is appropriate. Processor is not required to provide a root-cause analysis.

## 5. International Transfers
Processor may transfer Personal Data to countries without an adequacy decision. Standard Contractual Clauses are available only upon separate paid engagement.

## 6. Audit Rights
Controller may audit Processor no more than once every thirty-six (36) months, at Controller's expense, with sixty (60) days prior written notice, limited to SOC 2 Type I summary reports.

## 7. Deletion
Upon termination, Processor will delete Personal Data within one hundred eighty (180) days unless retention is required by law. Certification of deletion is not provided.
