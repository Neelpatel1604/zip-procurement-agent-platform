# Master Service Agreement — Northwind Software (Clean)
# Document ID: msa-northwind-clean
# Vendor: Northwind Software
# Risk profile: LOW

## 1. Parties
This MSA is between Customer and Northwind Software Corp. ("Provider"), domain northwind.software.

## 2. Limitation of Liability
Except for willful misconduct, gross negligence, IP indemnification, and confidentiality breaches, each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

## 3. Indemnification
Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Services infringe IP rights, and against claims arising from Provider's gross negligence causing a data breach. Mutual indemnification applies for each party's breach of confidentiality.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for convenience with thirty (30) days written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for material breach uncured within fifteen (15) days of notice. No early termination fee applies for convenience termination after the first twelve months.

## 5. Data Processing
Provider processes Personal Data only as documented in the DPA. Subprocessors are listed at Provider's trust portal; Customer may object to new subprocessors within fifteen (15) days. Data resides in the United States unless otherwise agreed.

## 6. Auto-Renewal
The Agreement renews for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the renewal date.

## 7. Insurance
Provider maintains cyber liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and will provide certificates upon request.
