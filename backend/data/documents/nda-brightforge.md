# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — BrightForge Labs
# Document ID: nda-brightforge
# Vendor: BrightForge Labs
# Risk profile: LOW

## 1. Purpose
BrightForge Labs Inc. ("Receiving Party"), domain brightforge.dev, and Customer ("Disclosing Party") wish to exchange confidential information for evaluating a potential software engagement.

## 2. Confidential Information
Confidential Information means non-public technical and business information disclosed in writing and marked confidential, or disclosed orally and confirmed in writing within fifteen (15) days.

## 3. Obligations
Receiving Party shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least reasonable care; (c) not disclose to third parties without prior written consent except to employees under confidentiality obligations who have a need to know.

## 4. Exclusions
Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of Receiving Party; (b) was already known; (c) is independently developed; (d) is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where legally permitted).

## 5. Term
This NDA remains in effect for three (3) years from the Effective Date. Surviving confidentiality obligations last two (2) years after termination.

## 6. Return or Destruction
Upon request, Receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing within thirty (30) days.

## 7. No License
Nothing in this NDA grants any license under patents, copyrights, or trade secrets.
